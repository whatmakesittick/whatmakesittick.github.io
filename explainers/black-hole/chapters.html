01

A hole in the sky

You are looking at a black hole the mass of millions of Suns, with a thin disc of hot gas circling it. Nothing you see is where it seems to be. The disc behind the hole appears bent over its top and under its bottom, and the stars behind it are smeared into a ring.

02

One side shines brighter

The gas in the disc moves at a good fraction of the speed of light. The side coming toward you is brighter and bluer, the side moving away is dimmer and redder.
